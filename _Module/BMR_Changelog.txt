BMR v4.9.0 :
- Re-added Militia Map Markers: Bandit Militias show a banner tracker on the map like your own parties and armies.
	New settings under UI & Notifications: Militia Map Markers (on) and Minimum BM Size To Track (50).
- Changed avoidance decay: each Bandit Militia now forgets avoidance once per week, regardless of the pillaging setting.
    Previously it only decayed with pillaging enabled and faster for militias with many neighbours.
- Fixed hero prisoners (e.g. captured lords) vanishing when a Bandit Militia split.
	They now move to the first new party.
- Fixed a waiting bandit hero being reused while still in an active party.
- Fixed Bonus XP on Training and Gold Reward on Hero Kill resetting to Normal on every launch.
- Fixed single items being tripled when a Bandit Militia split.
- Fixed Bandit Militia party names being rebuilt on every read instead of cached.
- Fixed Daily Split Chance: 0% now never splits and 100% always splits.
- Fixed a crash when a Bandit Militia looked for a village to raid and none was valid.
- Fixed the "raiding your village" message ignoring the Notify on Village Raid setting.
- Fixed a crash when a raid finished with no town within range.
- Fixed wounded troops being healed and troop XP being lost when a Bandit Militia split.
- Fixed two cancel options appearing when executing a Bandit Militia prisoner from the prison talk.
- Fixed raid messages showing blank village, party and town names.
	Text variables renamed to VILLAGE, PARTY and TOWN in all 13 languages.
	The "raiding your village" message now names the village's bound town.
- Fixed inventory items being lost when bandit parties merged into a Bandit Militia.
- Fixed Bandit Militias refusing to move toward friendly bandit parties they were trying to merge with.
	Strength and avoidance checks now apply to hostile targets only.
- Fixed banner icons of destroyed Bandit Militias staying in memory until the next load.
- Fixed deserter spawn logging building a stack trace while file logging was off.
- Fixed random banners being generated twice on every load.
- Updated the Simplified and Traditional Chinese translations with Cartoonist57's versions.
