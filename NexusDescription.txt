[color=#ffff00][size=4]Features[/size][/color]
[color=#ffd966]Bandit parties of the same clan that meet nearby merge into a Bandit Militia led by a bandit hero.[/color]
[color=#93c47d]Militia leaders are persistent heroes: they gain power, earn epithets like "the Bold" or "the Butcher" from victories and age, and get their own encyclopedia story.[/color]
[color=#ffd966]Large militias split into two; militias grow and train over time, up to a global power cap you choose.[/color]
[color=#93c47d]Militias patrol around their home hideout and raid rich villages nearby.[/color] ([color=#6aa84f]on[/color]/[color=#e06666]off[/color])
[color=#ffd966]Each militia remembers the lords who destroyed militias near it and avoids them. Beating a lord lowers that fear; it also fades every week.[/color]
[color=#93c47d]Defeat a militia leader to collect a bounty, then keep, release or execute your captive.[/color]
[color=#ffd966]Unique random banners for every militia, on the map and in battle.[/color] ([color=#6aa84f]on[/color]/[color=#e06666]off[/color])
[color=#93c47d]Map markers for large militias, like your own parties and armies.[/color] ([color=#6aa84f]on[/color]/[color=#e06666]off[/color])
[color=#ffd966]Messages when militias raid your villages.[/color] ([color=#6aa84f]on[/color]/[color=#e06666]off[/color])
[color=#93c47d]Just plug and play. No need to change anything for a great experience.[/color]
[color=#ffd966]Works with or without Mod Configuration Menu.[/color]
[color=#93c47d]Translations for 12 languages.[/color]

[color=#ffff00][size=4]Compatibility[/size][/color]
Can be added [color=#93c47d]anytime[/color]. To remove it, press [color=#f1c232]Ctrl+Alt+N[/color] on the campaign map and confirm to clear all mod data, then save, exit and disable the mod.
[color=#e06666]Adds[/color] data to save game files.
Uses Harmony patches for bandit AI, map visuals, prisoners and the encyclopedia; everything else uses campaign events.
If it loads after Calradia Expanded Kingdoms, [color=#ffd966]Random Banners[/color] is turned off automatically.
Works with: [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/10012]Adjustable Bandits (War Sails)[/url], [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/10304]Jomsborg Pirates For Warsails DLC[/url], [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/2969]Fourberie (Cunning)[/url], [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/3242]Serve as Soldier[/url] (1.4.7 or newer: its bait-run bandit party is left alone).
[color=#e06666]Possibly not compatible with:[/color] [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/4177]Bandit_Nation[/url], [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/2907]Realm of Thrones[/url], [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/8417]Shokuho[/url], [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/2304]The Original Player Switcher[/url] (Bandit Militia parties are [color=#e06666]not meant to be controlled[/color]).

[color=#ffff00][size=4]Installation[/size][/color]
Updating? [color=#e06666]Delete the old mod folder first[/color], never overwrite it.
Advisable to use [color=#ffd966]7zip[/color] to extract archive.
Put it in [color=#ffd966]Mount & Blade II Bannerlord[/color]\[color=#f1c232]Modules[/color] folder.
Enable the mod in the launcher. [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/2006]Harmony[/url] (and [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/612]Mod Configuration Menu[/url], if you use it) must be above it. Load it near the end of the load order.

[color=#ffff00][size=4]Found a problem?[/size][/color]
Generate a crash report link using [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/2018]ButterLib[/url] mod. How to do that the mini guide is here: [url=http://bannerlordmodding.lt/guides/how_to_report_a_crash/]How To Report A Crash[/url]
Console commands that help: [color=#f1c232]bmr.stats[/color] (militia counts and power), [color=#f1c232]bmr.heroes[/color] (every militia hero), [color=#f1c232]bmr.orphans[/color] (bandit heroes stuck outside militias), [color=#f1c232]bmr.fix_orphans[/color] (puts them back into the leader pool).
Turn on [color=#ffd966]Logging for debugging[/color] in [color=#ffd966]MCM[/color] settings or [color=#ffd966]ModConfiguration.xml[/color] in mod folder, [color=#f6b26b]repeat the problem[/color], then attach the file from [color=#ffd966]Modules[/color]\[color=#f1c232]BanditMilitiasRedux[/color]\[color=#ffd966]logs[/color] to your [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/2018]ButterLib[/url] crash report.
